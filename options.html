SubSearch Settings

Configure how SubSearch indexes and searches your YouTube subscriptions.

Indexing

How many recent videos to index per channel. Higher = more quota used. Recommended: 15–25.

ℹ Indexing all channels with the selected videos-per-channel count costs ~3 units per channel. Your daily limit is 10,000 units. Incremental syncs cost ~50–100 units. Video age is controlled by the freshness filter (max 5 years).

Search Defaults

Default filter shown when the overlay opens. Can be changed per-search.

Overlay

Open automatically when I search YouTube

When on, SubSearch opens over YouTube's results with your query prefilled — once you've built an index. Either way, the ⌕ button in the corner and Ctrl+Shift+F always open it.
