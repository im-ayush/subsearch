Indexing

How many recent videos to index per channel (5–200). Recommended: 15–25 for many subscriptions; go higher if you follow only a handful of channels and want their back catalogue.

ℹ Each channel costs 1 quota unit per 50 videos fetched — so 15 videos ≈ 1 unit and 200 videos ≈ 4 units per channel. Your daily limit is 10,000 units. Incremental syncs cost ~50–100 units. Video age is controlled by the freshness filter (max 5 years).
